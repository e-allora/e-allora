## What I'm building

Eleven products, each scored 0-100 on a [public rubric](https://shipitworks.com/how-we-score/), with what's not finished listed on every page. The full list, scores and demo videos are at [github.com/ShipItWorks](https://github.com/ShipItWorks) and [shipitworks.com](https://shipitworks.com).

A few of them:

- **[patchowner](https://shipitworks.com/patchowner/)**: exploited-vulnerability notices written for the person who has to act. For towns and small businesses with no security team.
- **[AI Consumer Rights](https://shipitworks.com/aiconsumerrights/)**: your rights when AI makes decisions about you, in plain words.
- **[Plainspoken](https://shipitworks.com/apps/#plainspoken)**: turns what you'd say into a prompt an AI understands, and shows you why.
- **[Graphsmith](https://shipitworks.com/apps/#graphsmith)**: design and test AI workflows as graphs before anything runs.
