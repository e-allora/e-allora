# Robert Sweetman

I'm Robert Sweetman, a union laborer turned software builder in Stoughton, Massachusetts. I'm building patchowner: exploited-vulnerability notices written for the person who has to act. Everything I make is scored out of 100 in public, with what is not done listed on the page.

## What I'm building

patchowner is the product I am working on. Ten more are paused, each scored 0-100 on a [public rubric](https://shipitworks.com/how-we-score/), with what's not finished listed on every page. The full list, scores and demo videos are at [github.com/ShipItWorks](https://github.com/ShipItWorks) and [shipitworks.com](https://shipitworks.com).

A few of them:

- **[patchowner](https://shipitworks.com/patchowner/)**: exploited-vulnerability notices written for the person who has to act. For towns and small businesses with no security team.
- **[AI Consumer Rights](https://shipitworks.com/aiconsumerrights/)** (paused): your rights when AI makes decisions about you, in plain words.
- **[Plainspoken](https://shipitworks.com/plainspoken/)** (paused): turns what you'd say into a prompt an AI understands, and shows you why.
- **[Graphsmith](https://shipitworks.com/graphsmith/)** (paused): design and test AI workflows as graphs before anything runs.

## How I work

I make the decisions. AI (mostly Claude, by Anthropic) writes most of the code, and every project says so. I say only what I can back, credit people and tools by name, collect as little data as possible, and fix mistakes in public.
